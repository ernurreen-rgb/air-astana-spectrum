# Fly Green MVP Scope

## UX Flow

1. Passenger selects a flight.
2. In the `Before You Fly` ancillary step, `Fly Green` appears next to seats and bags.
3. Passenger chooses one of three contribution tiers: Light, Balance, or Green+.
4. The total price, CO2 offset amount, Nomad Points, and Nomad Forest seed update immediately.
5. On continue, the passenger sees a confirmation receipt before payment.
6. After payment, the contribution is recorded in booking history and Nomad Club.

## Product Mechanics

- Light: low-friction entry tier, 30% estimated trip offset, 50 Nomad Points, Saxaul tree seed.
- Balance: default tier, 100% estimated trip offset, 150 Nomad Points.
- Green+: premium tier, double climate contribution with SAF-linked partner allocation, 300 Nomad Points.
- Nomad Forest turns each paid contribution into a visible seed so the benefit is remembered after purchase.

## MVP Included

- One embedded touchpoint in the booking ancillary flow.
- Three fixed contribution tiers.
- Dynamic total, CO2 amount, Nomad Points, and impact receipt.
- Local Nomad Forest prototype using browser storage.
- Simple analytics-ready events can be added around tier select, toggle, continue, and forest view.

## MVP Excluded

- Live payment integration.
- Route-by-route emissions calculator.
- Real certification registry integration.
- Corporate ESG reporting exports.
- Full SAF inventory allocation logic.

## Success Metrics

- Uptake scenarios: low 1%, base 3%, high 7%.
- Revenue or cost impact measured per 1,000 transactions.
- CO2 offset volume measured per 1,000 transactions.
- No extra step added to the booking/check-in flow.
